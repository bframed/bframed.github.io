Privacy Policy

Household Bills is designed as a local-first Windows application. Your household bill data stays on your device unless you choose to export it or open an external website.

English
In short: b-framed does not operate an account service or server that receives your bill records. The app contains no advertising or developer analytics.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Household Bills Windows application and its Household Bills Full Microsoft Store add-on, published by b-framed.

2. Information processed by the app

The app processes information that you choose to enter, such as bill amounts, categories, provider names, payment and due dates, meter readings, notes, reminders, preferences, and files attached to bill records.

This information may describe your household finances, but b-framed does not receive it.

3. Local storage

Your app data and attachments are stored locally in the application storage on your Windows device. Household Bills does not provide a b-framed account, cloud synchronization, or a b-framed-hosted backup service.

Anyone who can access your Windows account or device may be able to access locally stored or exported information. You are responsible for securing your device and exported files.

4. Export, backup, and printing

When you create a JSON backup, attachment ZIP, spreadsheet, PDF, or printed report, the action is initiated by you and the resulting file or printout may contain information you entered. You choose where exported files are saved and whether they are shared. b-framed does not receive these exports.

5. Internet access and third parties

  • Provider websites: if you open a website saved for a provider, it opens outside the app. That website’s own privacy policy then applies.
  • Microsoft Store: Microsoft may process account, purchase, payment, device, and license information to distribute the app and verify access to the Full add-on. Household Bills receives only the license or entitlement result needed to unlock features; b-framed does not receive your payment card details through the app.

For Microsoft’s processing, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The app does not include developer analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising, or sale of personal information. b-framed does not share your locally stored bill records because it does not receive them.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or reset the app’s data. Files that you exported or backed up must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved them. Uninstalling the app may not remove copies you created outside the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact b-framed through the support repository. Do not include bill details or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Pravila privatnosti

1. Opseg

Ova pravila primjenjuju se na Windows aplikaciju Household Bills i njezin dodatak Household Bills Full u Microsoft Storeu, čiji je izdavač b-framed.

2. Podaci koje aplikacija obrađuje

Aplikacija obrađuje podatke koje sami odlučite unijeti, primjerice iznose računa, kategorije, nazive pružatelja, datume plaćanja i dospijeća, očitanja brojila, bilješke, podsjetnike, postavke i datoteke priložene zapisima računa.

Ti podaci mogu opisivati financije vašeg kućanstva, ali ih b-framed ne prima.

Podaci i privici pohranjuju se lokalno u spremištu aplikacije na vašem Windows uređaju. Household Bills nema b-framed korisnički račun, sinkronizaciju u oblaku ni sigurnosnu pohranu na poslužiteljima b-frameda.

Osoba koja ima pristup vašem Windows računu ili uređaju može imati pristup lokalno spremljenim ili izvezenim podacima. Sami ste odgovorni za zaštitu uređaja i izvezenih datoteka.

4. Izvoz, sigurnosne kopije i ispis

Kada izradite JSON sigurnosnu kopiju, ZIP privitaka, tablicu, PDF ili ispisani izvještaj, radnju pokrećete vi, a rezultat može sadržavati podatke koje ste unijeli. Vi birate gdje se datoteke spremaju i hoćete li ih dijeliti. b-framed ne prima te izvoze.

5. Pristup internetu i treće strane

  • Microsoft Store: Microsoft može obrađivati podatke o računu, kupnji, plaćanju, uređaju i licenci radi distribucije aplikacije i provjere pristupa dodatku Full. Household Bills dobiva samo rezultat licence odnosno pravo pristupa potrebno za otključavanje funkcija; b-framed kroz aplikaciju ne prima podatke vaše platne kartice.

Za Microsoftovu obradu pogledajte Microsoftova pravila privatnosti.

6. Analitika, oglasi i dijeljenje

Aplikacija ne sadrži analitiku izdavača, praćenje ponašanja, oglase ni prodaju osobnih podataka. b-framed ne dijeli lokalno spremljene zapise računa jer ih ne prima.

7. Čuvanje i brisanje

Lokalni zapisi ostaju na uređaju dok ih ne izbrišete ili ponovno postavite podatke aplikacije. Izvezene datoteke i sigurnosne kopije morate zasebno izbrisati s mjesta na koje ste ih spremili. Deinstalacija aplikacije ne mora ukloniti kopije koje ste izradili izvan aplikacije.

8. Izmjene pravila

Ako se način obrade podataka u aplikaciji promijeni, ova će se stranica ažurirati i promijenit će se gore navedeni datum stupanja na snagu.

9. Kontakt
